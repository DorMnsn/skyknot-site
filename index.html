How to play

Hold anywhere on the screen to catch the marked star and swing around it, and let go to fly. Keep holding to loop: every full loop ties a knot that charges your next launch, and four knots make a Skyknot. Reach the top of the sky to clear the level.

Your progress

Skyknot has no accounts: your progress is saved on your phone. It doesn’t move to another phone, and uninstalling the game removes it. To start over, open Settings (the gear on Home) → Reset progress.
